# CineLUT Studio

> **Professional Cinematic Color Grading & 3D LUT Suite for Android**

CineLUT Studio is a professional cinema-grade color grading and video post-production suite designed for Android. It brings desktop-class grading workflows directly to mobile filmmakers, videographers, and creators — featuring real-time GPU-accelerated preview (OpenGL ES 3.0), native 3D `.cube` LUT support, and precision optical film effects (stochastic film grain, anamorphic halation, bloom diffusion, and custom aspect framing).

# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026  
**Application Name:** CineLUT Studio: Color Grading  
**Developer Contact:** [cinelutcontact@proton.me](mailto:cinelutcontact@proton.me)

CineLUT Studio is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data handling practices and confirms our core commitment: **your media, edits, and personal data remain strictly on your device.**

---

### 1. 100% Local On-Device Processing
CineLUT Studio operates entirely on your mobile device hardware. All media decoding, real-time 3D LUT application, color grading calculations, and final export rendering utilize local GPU hardware acceleration (OpenGL ES 3.0) and native Android MediaCodec. 

No video, frame, photo, audio track, or project file is ever uploaded, copied, analyzed, or stored on external cloud servers. All rendering and export pipelines run 100% offline.

---

### 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
We do not collect, harvest, sell, rent, or share any personal user data. 

The application does **not** require:
- User account creation, registration, or login
- Names, emails, phone numbers, or social media profiles
- Precise or approximate GPS location
- Financial or credit card information
- Biometric identifiers or contact lists

You can use CineLUT Studio without providing any personal identification.

---

### 3. On-Demand Technical Diagnostics
No automated telemetry, tracking cookies, or persistent background monitoring tools are embedded in CineLUT Studio.

If you experience a technical crash or issue and voluntarily choose the **"Generate & Send Diagnostics"** option inside the Settings menu:
- The app compiles a transient technical report containing strictly hardware specifications (device model, Android OS version, display resolution, GPU capabilities, and graphics pipeline log events).
- This report is handed to your device's default email client addressed to `cinelutcontact@proton.me`.
- **You have full visibility and control:** you can review the plain-text technical email before pressing send.
- This information is used strictly and exclusively for diagnosing bugs, crashes, and improving GPU compatibility.

---

### 4. Device Permissions & Media Storage Access
CineLUT Studio requests minimal device permissions strictly necessary for its functionality as a video editing tool:

- **Photos & Videos Access (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` / Storage Picker):** Used solely to let you browse and import video files from your device into the workspace for editing.
- **Public Storage (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / Scoped Storage `MediaStore`):** Used solely to save your rendered, finished master videos directly into your device's standard public `Movies/CineLUT` gallery.
- **Audio (`RECORD_AUDIO` / Passthrough):** Used exclusively for audio track passthrough and synchronization when rendering final video files. No microphone data is recorded, saved, or uploaded without your knowledge.

CineLUT Studio does not access your personal documents, contacts, or unselected media files.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & Analytics
CineLUT Studio does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioural trackers, or tracking pixels. Subscriptions and in-app purchases (if applicable) are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing via secure Google Play APIs. We do not store or process your financial data.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Because CineLUT Studio does not collect any personal data or transmit information over the Internet, the application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
